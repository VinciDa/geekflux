Frequently asked questions

Who is this guide for?

Students and working adults preparing for exams or learning dense material who want durable memory without burnout.

Do I need Anki?

No. Anki is excellent for high-volume facts. Many learners do better with topic-level revision schedules.

Which app pairs with this guide?

Revu — a spaced-repetition revision planner with calendar and reminders.
