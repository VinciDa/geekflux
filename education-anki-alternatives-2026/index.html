Anki alternatives in 2026: simpler ways to stick with spaced repetition.

Anki remains powerful. Abandonment remains common. Here is how to choose a spacing tool you will still open after the honeymoon.

By Maya Chen Published September 5, 2026 Read 11 min
M
Maya Chen
GeekFlux Research Desk — learning science, attention, and habit design for students and working adults.
Quick answer

Pick Anki for high-volume factual mastery and FSRS power-user control. Pick Quizlet for shared decks and casual study. Pick Brainscape for simple confidence ratings. Pick a revision planner like Revu when you want spaced topic reviews without building cards. Consistency beats algorithm bragging rights.

What “best” should mean in 2026

The best spaced-repetition tool is the one that still has a streak of opens in week six. Algorithm debates (FSRS vs SM-2) matter after consistency exists — not before.

FSRS vs SM-2 in one paragraph

SM-2 is the classic SuperMemo-derived schedule many apps inherited. FSRS is a newer model that uses more detailed review history to predict retention; Anki users often enable it for efficiency. Neither saves you if you never open the app.

Decision guide

  • Medical/language fact mountains → Anki (or an Anki-compatible modern client).
  • Class decks with friends → Quizlet first.
  • Hate complexity → Brainscape or a planner.
  • Multi-subject exam season without card time → revision planner (Revu’s lane).

The GeekFlux take

We care less about crowning a universal winner and more about matching the interface to the job. If flashcards are the job, pick a card app. If “revise Chapter 4 on Thursday before I forget” is the job, pick a planner.

Frequently asked questions

What is FSRS?

FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) is a modern scheduling algorithm used in Anki and some newer apps. It often aims for more efficient intervals than classic SM-2, though results depend on honest ratings and consistent use.

Is Anki free on iPhone?

AnkiMobile is a paid iOS app; desktop Anki is free. Cost and UI friction are common reasons people search for alternatives.

What is the best Anki alternative for beginners?

If you need shared decks quickly, Quizlet. If you want simple ratings, Brainscape. If you hate cards entirely, a revision planner.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Start with the lightest tool you will use daily. Migrate to Anki only if volume and customization demand it.
