# GeekFlux content roadmap — deferred & future phases

Companion to the September 2026 topical-authority expansion (Studying & Memory, Reading & Focus, Habits & Routines). This file is a publishing backlog, not a commitment to ship everything at once.

## Already shipped (this pass)

- 3 hub pages: `/studying-and-memory/`, `/reading-and-focus/`, `/habits-and-routines/`
- 13 cluster articles (5 study, 5 reading, 3 habits), including restored `/education-study-system/`
- Technical: `404.html`, `llms.txt`, `OAI-SearchBot` + `Claude-SearchBot` in `robots.txt`, GEO components (TL;DR, FAQ schema, author byline, inline CTAs)

## Phase 2 — seasonal & high-intent (priority order)

1. **January resolutions piece** (ship **November–December**): “New Year’s Resolutions Fail by January 19th — Here’s a System That Doesn’t” → Streakly. App Store indexing lag is 2–4 weeks; do not wait until 1 January.
2. **Exam deep-dives** (after timetable article gains traction):
   - US: SAT/ACT, MCAT, NCLEX, bar exam, PMP/CFA
   - UK: GCSE / A-Level by board (AQA, Edexcel, OCR)
   - AU: HSC / VCE / ATAR
   - IE/EU: Leaving Cert; IB where demand shows
3. **“Best Anki alternative for iPhone”** expanded comparison (Anki, Quizlet, Brainscape, RemNote, Mochi, Revu) once the planner-angle essay ranks.
4. **ADHD-adult reading** and format-specific pieces (Kindle / Libby / PDF) for ReadFast.
5. **Streakly widgets / Apple Watch** content — only after those features ship; do not invent them in copy.

## Phase 3 — product & architecture

- Cross-promote BITART apps (Timero/Pomodoro, Lexe/vocabulary) with dedicated clusters once angles are validated.
- Paginated `/articles/` archive if the homepage grid becomes unwieldy.
- Revisit India-specific posts’ role in primary navigation after the US/UK/EU/CA/AU pivot is measured in analytics (`page_view` + `store_click`).

## Phase 4 — off-site GEO (highest leverage outside the repo)

Brand-mention building on Reddit, niche forums, newsletters, and partner blogs. 2026 citation research correlates unlinked brand mentions more strongly with AI-engine visibility than classic backlinks alone. Pair with periodic refreshes of hub `dateModified` / “last reviewed” dates.

## Measurement checklist

- Track `store_click` by `app` (revu / readfast / streakly) and `placement` (sidebar vs `inline_cta`).
- Compare hub landing → store click rates vs culture/tech essay baselines.
- Re-test target queries in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, and Perplexity quarterly; refresh passages that fail to get cited.
